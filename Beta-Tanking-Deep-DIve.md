# World of Warcraft Forever: Comprehensive Tanking Class Analysis Report
## Threat Generation, Aggro Management & End-Game Raid Tanking Assessment

---

## Executive Summary

World of Warcraft Forever presents three primary tanking classes at launch: **Protection Warrior**, **Protection Paladin**, and **Feral Druid** (Bear form). This comprehensive analysis evaluates each specialisation's threat generation capabilities, aggro holding mechanics, and suitability for end-game raid tanking across three raid tiers: Barrow Deeps (10 players), Hyjal Summit (20 players), and Onyxia's Lair (40 players).

**Key Findings:**
- **Protection Warrior** emerges as the superior threat generator with predictable, reliable shield-based mechanics
- **Protection Paladin** offers exceptional versatility with multi-target threat and superior defensive capabilities
- **Feral Druid** provides unique mobility and flexibility but faces limitations in multi-target threat management

## Protection Warrior Analysis

### Overview

The Protection Warrior specialisation represents the classical tanking archetype within WoW Forever. Built around shield mastery and defensive stance, Warriors generate threat through active engagement with melee attackers and precise ability management.

### Core Threat Mechanics

#### Primary Threat Generation: Shield Slam
- **Resource Cost:** 20 Rage
- **Cooldown:** 6 seconds
- **Mechanics:** Damage scales with Block Value; generates substantial threat proportional to shield quality
- **Primary Use:** Main threat maintenance ability; foundation of rotation
- **Advantage:** High damage output + threat component creates efficient rage-to-threat ratio

#### Secondary Threat: Revenge
- **Trigger:** Activates after blocks, dodges, or parries
- **Threat Component:** Secondary threat generation during proc windows
- **Strategic Value:** Incentivises defensive talent choices that increase proc frequency

### Rage Generation System

Warriors generate rage through three primary mechanisms:

1. **Blocking** - Shield Specialisation talent provides "20% chance to generate 5 Rage when you Block"
2. **Dodging & Parrying** - Master of Defence talent grants "50% chance to generate 5 Rage on a dodge or parry"
3. **Melee Engagement** - Active tanking of melee attackers generates steady rage income

**Critical Limitation:** Rage generation depends entirely upon being struck by melee attacks. Off-tanks waiting for adds or preparing for tank swaps receive significantly reduced rage income, potentially causing threat generation gaps during transitions.

### Defensive Capabilities

#### Last Stand
- **Effect:** Temporarily grants 30% of maximum health for 20 seconds
- **Cooldown:** 3 minutes
- **Strategic Use:** Burst damage mitigation; raid cooldown coordination tool

#### Shield Wall
- **Effect:** Reduces incoming damage
- **Cooldown:** Improved from base (exact value in beta testing)
- **Note:** Separate cooldown from Last Stand; not continuous mitigation

### Key Talents & Synergies

| Talent | Effect | Strategic Value |
|---|---|---|
| **Defiance** | Increases threat in Defensive Stance whilst wielding shield | Core talent; mandatory for threat optimisation |
| **Bastion** | Increases damage output with shield equipped | Synergises with DPS output and threat generation |
| **Master of Defence** | 50% chance to generate 5 Rage on dodge/parry | Improves rage smoothing; increases offensive rotation frequency |
| **Shield Specialisation** | 20% chance to generate 5 Rage on block | Consistent passive rage generation |

### Critical Equipment Dependency

**Shield Requirement:** Warriors must remain shield-equipped to access Defiance threat bonus, Bastion damage bonus, and Master of Defence's dodge/parry mechanics.

**Consequence of Dual-Wielding:** Completely removes these defensive and offensive bonuses; unviable for tanking.

### Strengths
- ✓ Highest single-target threat generation
- ✓ Predictable, consistent threat output
- ✓ Excellent rage management through block-based systems
- ✓ Strong defensive cooldown suite
- ✓ Threat never depends on target movement or positioning

### Weaknesses
- ✗ Requires melee engagement to generate rage (problematic for off-tank positioning)
- ✗ Equipment dependent (shield requirement removes flexibility)
- ✗ Limited multi-target threat (moderate capability only)
- ✗ Defensive stance requirement limits repositioning speed
- ✗ Revenge proc dependency can create unpredictable threat windows

## Protection Paladin Analysis

### Overview

Protection Paladins represent a paradigm shift from traditional tanking. Rather than passive block-based mechanics, Paladins actively manage threat through the Seal of Fury system, Righteous Fury aura, and a sophisticated judgement-based taunt system. This creates a more resource-intensive but highly flexible threat model.

### Core Threat Mechanics

#### Primary Threat System: Seal of Fury + Judgement
- **Core Mechanic:** Judgement applies a taunt that functions only whilst Seal of Fury remains active
- **Resource:** Mana-based (not Rage)
- **Threat Component:** Judgement generates primary threat; Seal of Fury amplifies threat generation from subsequent abilities
- **Flexibility:** Swift Judgement talent resets taunt cooldown without changing seal

**Strategic Implication:** Paladins can maintain offensive damage output whilst simultaneously managing taunt responsibilities, unlike Warriors who sacrifice offensive potential during threat rotation.

#### Consecration
- **Threat Output:** Highest threat to first four enemies; reduced output beyond four targets
- **Resource:** Mana
- **Primary Use:** Multi-target threat generation; AoE tanking situations
- **Limitation:** Threat falloff beyond four enemies; requires positioning awareness in large add pulls

#### Holy Shield
- **Mechanics:** Generates 20% additional threat per block
- **Charges:** 4 charges per activation
- **Duration:** 10 seconds
- **Synergy:** Combines with Consecration for superior multi-target threat

#### Holy Strike
- **Mechanics:** Increased threat with Iron Creed talent
- **Talent Synergy:** Iron Creed provides 2-10% damage reduction on Holy Strike for 6 seconds (scaling with rank)
- **Strategic Role:** Alternative threat generation tool with defensive benefit

### Resource Management: Mana-Based Tanking

**Mana Return Mechanisms:**

1. **Improved Seal of Fury** - Restores mana "when that shield is fully consumed"
   - Requires proper Holy Shield usage for mana return
   - Creates dependency on block chance and shield charges

2. **Shield Specialisation** - Block-triggered mana restoration
   - Grants blocks a chance to restore 6% maximum mana
   - Maximum once every 3 seconds
   - Provides passive mana return for consistent uptime

### Defensive Capabilities

#### Holy Shield
- **Charges:** 4 per cast
- **Function:** Both offensive threat tool AND defensive ability
- **Dual Purpose:** Increases threat whilst absorbing damage

#### Templar's Bulwark
- **Effect:** Absorbs damage equal to maximum health
- **Strategic Role:** Significant cooldown for burst damage mitigation
- **Comparison:** Analogous to warrior Last Stand but with absorption mechanic

#### Iron Creed
- **Effect:** 2-10% damage reduction scaling with talent rank
- **Duration:** 6 seconds
- **Trigger:** Holy Strike activation
- **Strategic Value:** Offensive cooldown with defensive benefit

### Key Talents & Synergies

| Talent | Effect | Strategic Value |
|---|---|---|
| **Swift Judgement** | Resets Judgement cooldown without seal change | Enables continuous taunt availability; superior threat management |
| **Iron Creed** | 2-10% DR on Holy Strike for 6 seconds | Damage reduction scaling; tanking versatility |
| **Improved Seal of Fury** | Mana return on shield consumption | Enables mana-sustainable rotation |
| **Holy Shield** | Additional threat + block charges | Multi-target threat + defensive stacking |
| **Righteous Fury** | Aura requirement for taunt function | Mandatory passive buff |

### Critical System: Threat Tracking

Paladins must carefully manage:
- Holy Shield charge depletion tracking (separate from cooldown tracking)
- Seal of Fury status verification before taunting
- Mana pool sustainability during extended fights

**Common Mistakes to Avoid:**
- Pulling extra enemies solely to trigger mana return effects (healer burden increases significantly)
- Forgetting Seal of Fury active status, resulting in taunt failure
- Overextending Holy Shield usage without proper charge management

### Strengths
- ✓ Superior multi-target threat (highest to first four targets)
- ✓ Flexible threat generation (taunt resets + seal management)
- ✓ Mana-based resource enables sustained tanking during add phases
- ✓ Exceptional defensive cooldown stacking potential
- ✓ Can maintain offensive contribution whilst tanking

### Weaknesses
- ✗ Mana dependency creates resource management complexity
- ✗ Threat effectiveness diminishes beyond four targets
- ✗ Requires seal tracking (mechanical overhead)
- ✗ Cooldown-dependent taunt (not always available)
- ✗ Defensive cooldowns require talent investment and management

## Feral Druid (Bear Form) Analysis

### Overview

Feral Druids employ a fundamentally different tanking philosophy. Rather than constant shield management or seal maintenance, Druids rely on powerful cooldown windows (Berserk) and adaptive defensive positioning. This creates a highly mobile but precision-dependent tanking experience.

### Core Threat Mechanics

#### Primary Threat: Mangle During Berserk

**Berserk Effect:**
- **Duration:** 15 seconds
- **Primary Benefit:** Removes Mangle's cooldown
- **Multi-Target Capability:** Mangle strikes up to three targets simultaneously
- **Resource Cost:** Mangle still costs 20 Rage despite cooldown removal
- **Critical Limitation:** Empty rage bar prevents Mangle execution; not a free-spam ability

**Strategic Implication:** Berserk defines the threat rotation; Druids must budget Rage consumption carefully to maximise Mangle strikes within the 15-second window.

#### Secondary Threat Abilities

**Swipe**
- **Targets:** Hits three nearby enemies
- **Primary Use:** Multi-target threat during Berserk; maintains threat on multiple adds
- **Positioning Requirement:** Must remain in melee range of all three targets

**Growl**
- **Mechanics:** Single-target taunt
- **Limitation:** Only effective if target not already attacking the Druid
- **Strategic Role:** Add pickup; threat management for strays

**Challenging Roar**
- **Effect:** Forces nearby enemies to focus the Druid for 6 seconds
- **Positioning:** Requires proximity to target enemies
- **Tactical Use:** Controlled add management; threat correction

### Rage Generation System

Druids generate Rage through three specialised mechanics:

1. **Natural Reaction** - Dodge-triggered Rage
   - Grants up to 5 Rage per dodge
   - Scales with dodge chance and enemy attack frequency
   - Highly variable in non-combat scenarios

2. **Primal Fury** - Critical Strike Rage
   - Grants Rage when critical strikes land in Bear form
   - Scales with critical strike chance
   - Incentivises critical strike itemisation

3. **Furor** - Shift Talent
   - Grants guaranteed 10 Rage when shifting into Bear form
   - Provides reliable Rage pool at engagement start
   - Enables immediate threat generation

### Defensive Capabilities

#### Survival Mitigation
- **Armour Role:** Critical for physical damage but ineffective against magical damage spikes
- **Positioning Matter:** Placing correctly avoids multiple damage sources simultaneously
- **Consequence:** Magic damage encounters require different mitigation strategies than physical threats

#### Defensive Cooldowns
- **Cooldown Availability:** Not extensively detailed in current beta documentation
- **Positioning Trade-offs:** Defensive abilities must balance threat generation against survival needs

### Key Talents & Synergies

| Talent | Effect | Strategic Value |
|---|---|---|
| **Furor** | 10 Rage on Bear form shift | Reliable threat generation start; enables opener rotation |
| **Natural Reaction** | Dodge-triggered Rage (up to 5) | Passive Rage generation; scales with avoidance stats |
| **Primal Fury** | Critical strike triggered Rage | Synergises with DPS itemisation |
| **Berserk** | Removes Mangle cooldown (15 seconds) | Core cooldown; defines threat rotation |

### Tactical Positioning Considerations

**Critical Positioning Mechanic:** Three-target Mangle does not secure a fourth or fifth enemy on its own.

**Requirements for Large Pulls:**
- Coordinate with damage dealers on target focus
- Manage adds outside attack range through Challenging Roar
- Maintain positioning that keeps maximum targets within Swipe range
- Use Growl for strays beyond primary threat range

**Consequence:** Requires coordinated raid communication; threat control is not purely tank-driven.

### Multi-Target Threat Limitation

Unlike Warriors (single-target) or Paladins (four-target), Druids cap multi-target threat at three enemies simultaneously. Larger pulls require exceptional positioning or damage dealer coordination to prevent adds from peeling.

### Strengths
- ✓ Exceptional single-target threat during Berserk window
- ✓ Superior mobility compared to plate-wearers
- ✓ Adaptive positioning; not locked into defensive stance
- ✓ Flexibility to shift roles if needed (healing, DPS)
- ✓ Threat independent of target positioning or movement

### Weaknesses
- ✗ Limited to three-target threat ceiling
- ✗ Berserk-dependent rotation creates predictable, cooldown-reliant tanking
- ✗ Rage dependency on dodges/crits (less predictable than block-based)
- ✗ Magic damage vulnerability (armour provides no protection)
- ✗ Requires precise positioning and damage dealer coordination

## Threat Generation Mechanics

### Threat Metres and Aggro Control

WoW Forever employs a threat-based tanking system (not a threat meter display system per beta data). The following mechanics govern threat holding:

1. **Ability Threat Coefficients**
   - Each ability generates threat proportional to damage dealt
   - Tank abilities include threat multipliers
   - Damage dealers generate threat on enemy targets proportional to damage output

2. **Threat Tables**
   - Tank occupies primary position with highest threat
   - Enemies attack highest threat target
   - Threat drops when tank ceases damage output (off-tank scenarios)

3. **Taunt Mechanics**
   - Paladins' Judgement (with Seal of Fury) functions as taunt
   - Druids' Growl functions as single-target taunt
   - Warriors lack taunt abilities (must maintain highest threat through damage)

**Implication:** Warriors cannot recover from threat loss; must maintain continuous highest threat. Paladins and Druids have tactical recovery options.

### Threat Generation Formulae (Inferred from Ability Mechanics)

**Protection Warrior:**
```
Threat = (Shield Slam Damage × Threat Multiplier) + (Revenge Proc × Threat Multiplier)
Rage Income = (Block Chance × 0.20) + (Dodge/Parry Chance × 0.50)
```

**Protection Paladin:**
```
Threat = (Judgement Threat) + (Consecration Damage × Threat Multiplier) + (Holy Shield Damage × 1.20)
Mana Sustainability = (Shield Consumption Return) + (Block Chance × 0.06 × Max Mana / 3 seconds)
```

**Feral Druid:**
```
Threat (Berserk) = (Mangle Damage × Threat Multiplier × Number of Targets up to 3)
Threat (Non-Berserk) = (Swipe × Threat Multiplier) + (Growl Threat Reset)
Rage Income = (Dodge Chance × up to 5) + (Critical Strike Chance × Rage) + (10 Rage Shift)
```

## Aggro Holding Strategies

### Protection Warrior Strategy

**Opening Rotation:**
1. Activate Defensive Stance
2. Equip shield (maximum Block Value)
3. Generate initial Rage through passive incoming damage
4. Cast Shield Slam on primary target (20 Rage)
5. Trigger Revenge procs through blocking/dodging

**Maintenance Rotation:**
- Prioritise Shield Slam cooldown every 6 seconds
- Maintain Revenge usage on proc
- Manage Rage for unplanned adds
- Monitor Block Value upgrades to maximise threat output

**Add Management:**
- Limited multi-target capability
- Requires fast positioning on strays
- Consider Revenge priority on secondary targets if Rage permits

**Threat Recovery:**
- No taunt capability; threat must be maintained through damage
- Requires immediate aggression on threat-loss scenarios
- Off-tank scenarios problematic due to Rage generation dependency

### Protection Paladin Strategy

**Opening Rotation:**
1. Activate Righteous Fury (mandatory for taunt function)
2. Activate Seal of Fury
3. Cast Judgement on primary target (taunt + threat)
4. Layer Holy Shield (4 charges)
5. Execute Holy Strike with Iron Creed

**Maintenance Rotation:**
- Maintain Seal of Fury status
- Reset Judgement via Swift Judgement talent (prioritise)
- Sustain Holy Shield charges
- Use Consecration for multi-target threat
- Monitor mana; trigger Improved Seal return if available

**Add Management:**
- Consecration provides superior multi-target threat (first four targets)
- Position to maximise add clustering
- Use Judgement taunt for additional adds if necessary
- Holy Shield stacking prevents threat loss

**Threat Recovery:**
- Swift Judgement enables rapid taunt resets
- Superior threat recovery compared to Warriors
- Off-tank scenarios handled well through taunt management

### Feral Druid Strategy

**Opening Rotation:**
1. Activate Bear form (generates 10 Rage via Furor)
2. Engage primary target
3. Generate additional Rage through dodges/crits
4. Coordinate with raid on target focus
5. Trigger Berserk at optimal Rage pool (60-80 Rage)

**Berserk Window (15 seconds):**
1. Spam Mangle on primary target (20 Rage per cast; no cooldown)
2. Alternate Swipe every 2-3 Mangles (hits three targets)
3. Maintain Challenging Roar for strays
4. Maximise threat generation before cooldown expires

**Maintenance Rotation (Non-Berserk):**
- Use Swipe on multiple targets
- Cast Growl on strays
- Manage positioning to keep adds in melee range
- Prepare for next Berserk window

**Threat Recovery:**
- Growl provides taunt but limited utility (only if target not attacking)
- Challenging Roar forces focus (group cooldown)
- Requires damage dealer coordination for true threat management

**Critical Constraint:** Threat ceiling at three targets without exceptional positioning and damage dealer focus.

## End-Game Raid Recommendations

### Raid Composition Strategy

**Barrow Deeps (10 Players):** Single Tank Requirement

**Optimal Composition:**
- **Primary Tank:** Protection Warrior or Feral Druid
- **Rationale:** Single-target threat dominance; predictable damage patterns in 10-player content
- **Secondary Consideration:** Protection Paladin viable if encounter features adds (superior multi-target)

**Warrior Advantage:** Highest threat ceiling; minimal add management requirements; most consistent performance

**Paladin Consideration:** Superior add handling if encounters feature multiple spawn phases

**Druid Consideration:** Superior mobility for positional mechanics; Berserk window timing critical

---

**Hyjal Summit (20 Players):** Likely Dual-Tank Requirement

**Optimal Composition:**
- **Primary Tank:** Protection Warrior (boss tanking)
- **Secondary Tank:** Protection Paladin (add tanking + swaps)

**Rationale:** Warriors provide superior single-target threat for main boss. Paladins handle multi-target add phases with exceptional Consecration output. Ability to taunt enables smooth tank swaps.

**Alternative Composition:**
- **Primary Tank:** Protection Paladin (exceptional defensive cooldown stacking)
- **Secondary Tank:** Protection Warrior (backup threat)
- **Advantage:** Superior add management throughout encounter

**Druid Consideration:** Provides unique mobility for positional mechanics; likely secondary role due to limited multi-target

---

**Onyxia's Lair (40 Players):** Dual or Multiple Tank Requirement

**Optimal Composition:**
- **Primary Tank:** Protection Warrior (main tank boss phase)
- **Secondary Tanks:** 1-2 Protection Paladins (add handling, cooldown rotation, phase transitions)
- **Tertiary Tank:** Feral Druid (mobility for flying phases; add pickup)

**Rationale:** 
- Warriors maintain primary boss threat
- Paladins enable sophisticated add management (five+ adds common in 40-player content)
- Druids provide adaptability for positional or role-transition mechanics

**Alternative Composition for Add-Heavy Encounters:**
- **Primary Tank:** Protection Paladin (superior multi-target)
- **Secondary Tanks:** 1-2 Protection Warriors (single-target backup)
- **Tertiary Tank:** Feral Druid (positional mechanics)

**Critical Consideration:** Raid composition flexibility; WoW Forever encourages multiple viable tank combinations rather than single optimal setup.

- **Raid Viability:** Excellent for Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit, and Onyxia's Lair

**Secondary Choice: Dwarf**
- **Stoneform Racial:** Reduces physical damage and grants immunity to bleeds, poisons, diseases (8 seconds)

- **Big Game Hunter:** Additional damage against Beast-type enemies
- **Synergy:** Physical damage reduction pairs excellently with Shield Wall and defensive stance
- **Application:** Superior for physical damage-heavy encounters; excellent disease immunity
- **Raid Viability:** Strong for raids with poison/disease mechanics

**Tertiary Choice: Human**
- **Sword Specialisation:** Grants crit with one- or two-handed swords
- **Will to Survive:** Removes stun effects
- **Synergy:** Stun removal assists threat maintenance against crowd control
- **Application:** Helpful for PvP-adjacent mechanics; modest threat boost
- **Raid Viability:** Moderate for pure tanking; stronger for threat generation

- **Orc:** Axe Specialisation (crit), Blood Fury (attack power), Hardiness (20% stun reduction) — Good offensive tanking

- **Undead:** Will of the Forsaken (removes fear/charm/sleep), Cannibalize (mana/health recovery) — Control removal utility
- **Gnome:** Escape Artist (mobility), Eureka (40% ability cost reduction, 10% damage increase) — Niche utility
- **Troll:** Attack speed burst, health recovery between enemies — Moderate threat boost
- **Skyborne (Windshaper):** 1% haste, elemental damage resistance — Specialised for elemental encounters

#### PvP (World PvP & Battlegrounds)

**Best Choice: Orc**
- **Hardiness Racial:** Reduces stun duration by 20%
- **Blood Fury:** Temporarily increases attack power and removes curses
- **Synergy:** Stun reduction critically important for maintaining threat against enemy crowd control
- **Application:** Survives burst crowd control from enemy casters; maintains offensive pressure
- **PvP Viability:** Superior survivability in chaotic PvP scenarios

**Secondary Choice: Undead**
- **Will of the Forsaken:** Removes charm, fear, and sleep effects
- **Cannibalize:** Restores health and mana between encounters
- **Synergy:** Multiple crowd control removals enable continuous threat maintenance
- **Application:** Excellent against caster-heavy opposition; mana recovery supports extended fights
- **PvP Viability:** Superior control removal options

- **Endurance:** Extra health provides survivability buffer
- **Synergy:** Crowd control ability + health pool enables tanking multiple opponents

- **PvP Viability:** Good for world PvP; weaker in organised battlegrounds

- **Combined Effect:** Easiest levelling experience through enhanced survivability

**Secondary Choice: Dwarf**
- **Stoneform:** Poison/disease immunity prevents debuff downtime
- **Mace Specialisation:** Early crit bonus improves damage output
- **Combined Effect:** Cleaner combat without status effects
- **Levelling Speed:** Good for consistent progress

**Tertiary Choice: Orc**
- **Blood Fury:** Attack power boost improves levelling damage output
- **Hardiness:** Stun reduction prevents mob crowd control
- **Combined Effect:** Strong offensive levelling; good survival
